# Student Study Hours Analysis

## Objective
To analyze whether study hours affect students' internal marks.

## Data
The project uses:
- Student Name
- Study Hours per Day
- Attendance (%)
- Internal Marks

## Tools Used
- Microsoft Excel / Google Sheets
- GitHub

## Analysis
- Average study hours per day: 3.5 hours
- A bar chart shows study hours for each student.
- A pie chart shows the distribution of students by study-hour category.
- In this sample, students who study more hours generally have higher internal marks.

## Conclusion
Based on the sample data, students who spend more time studying generally have higher internal marks. However, study hours are not the only factor that may affect academic performance; attendance and other factors can also contribute.
